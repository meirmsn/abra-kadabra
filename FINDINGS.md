# Findings

What Claude noticed while working on the devshop, or in a project that uses it, and didn't fix,
kept so the owner can watch what comes back. A finding is not a task: what the owner decided to do
is in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). The rules are in the template,
[runtime/templates/findings.md](runtime/templates/findings.md) ([spec](specs/findings-and-roadmap.md)).
In a devshop session, findings are written here directly; from a project session they arrive in the
block the owner pastes. IDs carry on from the archive, `F-NEW-<n>`; the findings before 2026-10-16,
closed ones included, are in [kit/notes/revamp/FINDINGS.md](kit/notes/revamp/FINDINGS.md).
