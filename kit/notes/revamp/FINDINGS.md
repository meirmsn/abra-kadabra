# Findings register

Evidence base for the revamp. Every finding has an ID, a source, a confidence
mark, and the task(s) that resolve it. **Coverage rule:** every finding maps to
≥1 task ID or is explicitly marked `won't fix` / `obsolete by <task>` with a reason.
The plan review session checks this ([PROCESS.md § 5](PROCESS.md#plan-review-session-next)).

Confidence: **V** = verified by reading code / running it / official docs in the
analysis session · **R** = reported by a review subagent, not independently
re-verified (verify before acting) · **?** = plausible, unconfirmed.
REVIEW-1 (2026-10-08) re-checked every V row and most R rows; confidence and
wording below reflect that pass ([REVIEW-1.md](REVIEW-1.md)).

> **After D-14 (2026-10-09):** most `→ Task` targets below are v1 tasks now marked `[-]`
> (superseded by the rewrite). Their findings are not dropped: the **v2 disposition**
> column (W10.3.3, 2026-10-12) gives every row exactly one of: `v2 requirement → <pillar>`
> (with the [lessons.md](lessons.md) `L-nn` and [TARGET](TARGET.md) L3 bullet that carry it),
> `not applicable to v2 (<reason>)`, or `won't fix — v1 retired` (with what replaced or
> dropped the v1 part). Live exposure is handled now by W0.5–W0.7. New `F-NEW-nn` rows
> get a disposition when they are added.

Paths are relative to the repo root. Line numbers are as of commit `e0f082a` (v1.38.0).

New findings discovered during the revamp are appended at the bottom under
**Discovered during revamp**, IDs `F-NEW-nn`, with "found during <task>".
