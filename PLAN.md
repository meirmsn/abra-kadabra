### Scoring axes (legend)

The order below derives from a per-row scoring pass. Stored here so a future session can re-score a new row consistently OR contest an existing slot without re-deriving the rubric.

- **Score** = impact × risk_if_not ÷ cost-divisor. Higher = sooner. Tie-break on dependencies (see § Dependency reorderings below).
